TEMP TABLESPACE ORACLE
======================
Utilisé pour:
- Tris (ORDER BY, GROUP BY)
- Joins (HASH JOIN, SORT MERGE)
- CREATE INDEX
- Requêtes analytiques (RANK, ROW_NUMBER)

Monitoring:
SELECT tablespace_name, SUM(bytes_used)/1024/1024 AS mb_used
FROM v$temp_extent_pool
GROUP BY tablespace_name;

Si plein:
- Optimiser requêtes (réduire tris)
- Augmenter PGA_AGGREGATE_TARGET
- Étendre TEMP tablespace

ALTER TABLESPACE temp ADD TEMPFILE '/u01/data/temp02.dbf' SIZE 2G;
